FROSTFILE — Family Credit Freeze Kit
=====================================

This drive was given to you by someone who knows you. It contains a free
program that helps you freeze your family's credit — the single most
effective thing you can do against identity theft — and keep track of the
PINs, confirmation numbers, and paperwork that come with it.

Everything stays on YOUR computer. The program never touches the internet.
No account, no subscription, no catch.


WINDOWS
-------
1. Copy FrostFile-windows.zip from this drive onto your computer (the
   Desktop is fine — anywhere you'll find it again).
2. Right-click the zip -> "Extract All..." -> Extract. You get a folder
   called FrostFile.
3. Open that folder and double-click FrostFile.exe (the snowflake icon).
4. Windows may show a blue box: "Windows protected your PC." That warning
   appears for ANY new program that hasn't paid Microsoft for a publisher
   certificate — it is not a virus detection. Click "More info", then
   "Run anyway."
5. FrostFile opens and walks you through the rest.

IF YOUR ANTIVIRUS COMPLAINS
---------------------------
Some antivirus programs quarantine ALL new, uncertified software on sight —
reputation, not analysis. The honest facts: FrostFile is brand new, free,
and unsigned (certificates cost hundreds a year), so it has no reputation
yet. What it does have: public source code (github.com/wingding-dev/
frostfile), published fingerprints (SHA256SUMS.txt on this drive — your
copy can be checked against them), and zero internet connections, which
you can prove by turning off Wi-Fi and watching it work. If you're
comfortable with that, restore the file from quarantine and choose
"allow" / "trust". If you're not, that's okay too — ask the person who
gave you this drive to sit with you the first time.

MAC
---
1. Copy FrostFile-mac.zip onto your computer and double-click to unzip.
2. Right-click (or hold Control and click) FrostFile.app -> Open -> Open.
   (Plain double-clicking gets blocked the first time because the app
   isn't in Apple's store. Right-click -> Open is the official way around
   that, and you only need it once.)

IMPORTANT — THE TWO THINGS TO NEVER LOSE
----------------------------------------
When you set up, FrostFile asks you to create a passphrase and then shows
you a RECOVERY CODE — print it or write it down and put it somewhere safe
(with your passports is a good spot). Your information is scrambled with
your passphrase; nobody — not the person who gave you this drive, not
anybody — can recover it if you lose both the passphrase and that code.
That's not a flaw. That's the point.
