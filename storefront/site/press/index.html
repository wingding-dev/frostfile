Descriptions

One line:

FrostFile — free, open-source tracker for your family's credit freezes. Runs offline on your own computer. Windows & Mac.

Short:

FrostFile is a free, open-source program that tracks your whole family's credit freezes — the three big bureaus plus ~20 agencies most people never hear of — and prints the mail-in packets for children's freezes. It runs on your own computer and makes zero internet connections. Windows 10+ and macOS 11+.

Long:

A credit freeze is a deadbolt — it stops someone opening an account in your name. Credit monitoring is a doorbell camera; it only tells you afterward. FrostFile helps a family throw the deadbolt everywhere it counts: Equifax, Experian and TransUnion, plus the specialty agencies (Innovis, ChexSystems, LexisNexis, NCTUE, The Work Number and more) and the government controls that stop account-takeover (IRS IP PIN, Social Security, E-Verify Self Lock, USPS Informed Delivery). It tracks status, PINs and confirmation numbers, prints the mail-in packets children's freezes require, reminds you when yearly tasks come due, and shows what changed between saved credit reports. Every fact links to an official source. It stores everything in one encrypted file on your computer and contains no code capable of making a network connection — an automated test fails the build if anyone adds one. Free forever, AGPL-licensed, no account. Built by one parent after a data breach, then given away.
