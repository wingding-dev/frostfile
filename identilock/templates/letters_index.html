Mailing Packets

Freezing a child's credit is free and federally guaranteed. At the three big bureaus it can't be done online — the documents they require make it mail-only{{ cite(citer, refs('ca-ag-child-freeze')) }}, and that paperwork is the reason most families never finish. Identilock prints the cover letter and the checklist; you supply the copies and the stamp. (A few agencies do accept a child's freeze online or by phone — those are noted on their own pages.)
