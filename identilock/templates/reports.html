How the comparison works — and its limits.

The bureaus only hand out reports as documents meant to be read by eye, and each bureau lays its paperwork out differently — so no program can read one perfectly, this one included. Identilock goes through the words in the file and picks out what looks like account numbers, addresses, employers, and companies that checked your credit, then shows you what has appeared since your last copy.

That summary can miss things, and it can flag harmless changes. So every comparison also lets you see the two copies side by side, line by line, with nothing filtered out. If the summary and the line-by-line view disagree, trust the line-by-line view.

Add a Report

A PDF, a text file, or a web page saved from your browser. The report never goes to the internet — Identilock keeps only the words from the file, scrambled (encrypted) into its database on this computer. A scanned or photographed report will not work, because a program cannot read the words out of a picture.
