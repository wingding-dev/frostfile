Sources

Every address, phone number, and how-to claim in this app comes from one of the pages below. The app never looks anything up while it runs — this list was put together by hand on {{ compiled_on }} and ships with the app, so you can check any line of it yourself.

What the two colors mean.

✓ read on the date shown (green) — a person actually opened this page on that date and copied the facts straight out of it. A mailing address has to meet this bar before FrostFile will print a mailing packet for it.

link only — go check (yellow) — this is the organization's own page for the topic, but nobody recorded what it actually says. Yellow does not mean the information is wrong; it means “open the page and see for yourself before counting on it.”

Are These Links Still Alive?

Every link below was opened and confirmed working on {{ links_verified_on }}, as part of preparing the version of FrostFile you are running — and that check happens again for every new version. The app itself never checks: FrostFile makes zero internet connections, so a link can only be tested by a person before release, or by you clicking it. A working link proves the page is still there — not that it still says the same thing — which is why mailing addresses always get the “checked today, by you” treatment on the printed checklist.

If You Find Something Wrong

These organizations move their PO boxes and reshuffle their websites without telling anyone. If a link is dead or an address looks off, do not mail anything to it — check the organization's own site, and tell whoever gave you this app so the fix reaches everyone.
