What the two colors mean.

✓ read on the date shown (green) — a person actually opened this page on that date and copied the facts straight out of it. A mailing address has to meet this bar before FrostFile will print a mailing packet for it.

link only — go check (yellow) — this is the organization's own page for the topic, but nobody recorded what it actually says. Yellow does not mean the information is wrong; it means “open the page and see for yourself before counting on it.”
