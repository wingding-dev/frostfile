If Something Turns Up

  • An email in leaks: normal for most people — what matters is which data leaked. Passwords or Social Security numbers leaked means act; just an email address means stay alert for phishing.
  • A leaked password: change it everywhere you used it, starting with email and banking. Never reuse it again.
  • SSN in a leak: a leaked SSN is what freezes and the IRS IP PIN exist for — both are already on your grid.

Results are between you and that website — FrostFile doesn't see or store them. If you want a record, add a note on the person's page.
