⚠ Double-check every address yourself before mailing — no exceptions.

These envelopes hold birth certificates and Social Security cards. Agencies move their PO boxes without telling anyone, and a packet mailed to a stale address is like handing it to a stranger — worse than not mailing it at all. Before sealing each envelope, open that agency's official page and compare its address to the printed one, line by line. The app's green checkmarks mean “verified at some point,” never “skip checking today.”

Send copies of documents, never originals, and use a separate envelope per agency per child — each checklist page says exactly what goes in its envelope and where it is mailed.
